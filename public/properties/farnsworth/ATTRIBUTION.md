# Edith Farnsworth House reference assets

All six selected assets depict the same physical residence: Edith Farnsworth House, 14520 River Road, Plano, Illinois, United States. The home is now a house museum. The collection is an archival reference set, not a present-day property inspection or a scan.

## Mapping limitations

The main space is open plan. “Dining”, “living”, “sleeping” and “circulation” refer to zones labelled in or interpreted from the measured plan. The internal workflow ID `hallway` denotes open-plan circulation and does not assert an enclosed hallway. Kitchen and guest/master bathroom geometry is present in the measured floor plan, but this selected colour-photo subset contains no kitchen or bathroom image. No unrelated photograph substitutes for those missing views.

Furniture and zone coordinates in Caretakr's procedural interpretation are approximate. Synthetic meter records or maintenance scenarios, if shown, must remain separately labelled and must never be attributed to an apparent defect in these archival photographs. No museum, photographer, institution or architect endorsement is implied.
