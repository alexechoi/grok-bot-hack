# Caretakr visual direction

Caretakr is a spatial inspection experience. The apartment and its working agents lead; the interface provides a small, legible set of controls around them. The audience is property operators and hackathon viewers watching a normal screen recording.

## Palette

- Charcoal stage: `#25272b`
- Raised charcoal surface: `#30343b`
- Warm paper: `#f2f0e7`
- Secondary ink on charcoal: `#b4bcc6`
- Muted blue action: `#acc4dc`
- Divider: `#ffffff14`

Blue, violet, orange and green belong to the four agent identities. Severity colours belong to operational alerts. The kitchen's violet overlay communicates the energy anomaly, not decoration.

## Type and rhythm

Geist is the interface face: precise, understated and readable beside a miniature architectural model. Use 14px actions and 12px supporting controls; delete secondary wording before shrinking type. The property name is 24px, contextual headings 24–30px. Use sentence case and natural letter spacing. Measurements and timestamps use tabular numerals.

Spacing follows a 4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Small controls use 8px radii; contextual surfaces use 12px. One elevation cue per surface. No ornamental gradient, glow, glass blur, kicker or redundant numbered badge.

## Composition

The full-viewport diorama is the memorable element. A compact roster sits at the left edge, camera controls at the right, and the inspection action at the lower right. The house is composed slightly right of centre on desktop and fitted to the available width on mobile. Ground and background are seamless charcoal; illumination and soft ambient contact create depth without a hard cast shadow across the stage.

Idle characters stay at their assigned positions. Travel, investigation, report packets, markers and completion respond to real backend workflow events. In Demo those events are clearly synthetic. Respect reduced motion for ambient movement and automatic camera tracking.

## Details

Evidence, reasoning summaries and configuration are disclosed on demand. A report is an outcome, not the main screen. Preserve source IDs and execution provenance in every report. Keyboard focus, labelled controls, trapped dialog focus, safe-area spacing and touch targets are part of the visual quality floor.
